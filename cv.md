### 2021–present · Director of Data Access & Partnerships, NHS England (formerly NHS Digital)
Run NHS England's external data access and clinical trials services —
roughly £25m a year, 100+ people. That covers the Secure Data Environment
(safe access to billions of rows of de-identified patient data for
academic and commercial researchers), NHS DigiTrials (recruited 1.5m+
people into trials, including NHS Galleri, the UK's fastest-recruiting
randomised trial), and around 1,000 live data-sharing agreements.

### 2019–2021 · Director of Health Informatics, Health Data Research UK, Cambridge
Set the health informatics strategy for HDR UK's Cambridge site — a
partnership between the Wellcome Sanger Institute, EBI, Cambridge and its
hospitals. Built the team, delivered data linkages for a cohort of 325,000
blood donors, helped establish the Polygenic Score Catalog, and led health
informatics for COVID-19 Genomics UK (COG-UK), including moving fast
during the pandemic to link viral genetic sequences to patient data and
secure philanthropic funding for it.

### 2013–2019 · Progressively senior roles, Cancer Research UK
Project Director for the Cancer Patient Portal, then Head of Cancer
Intelligence, then Director of Information & Patient Involvement — ending
up running a 150+ person department covering health, patient and
statistical evidence. Advised the CEO and board, set CRUK's patient
involvement strategy, and co-led the review of cancer registry opt-outs
that shaped the National Data Opt-out.

### 2008–2013 · Senior Research Programme Manager, National Cancer Intelligence Network / NCRI
Opened up researcher access to linked cancer registry data for the first
time, wrote a widely-used template for data and tissue access policies,
and represented NCRI on national data access and human tissue issues. See
[Work](/work/) for some of what came out of this period.

### 2006–2008 · Senior Consultant, Ernst & Young
Pricing, commercial strategy and cost-reduction work for corporate
clients, before moving into health data.

### Earlier
PhD in Biochemistry, University of Cambridge (BBSRC/GSK-funded); BA/MSci,
also Cambridge, First Class Honours.
