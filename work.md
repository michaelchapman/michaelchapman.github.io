Reports and briefings from earlier in my career. Most of these were
originally published by organisations that no longer exist in that form
(NCIN and NIGB were both folded into other bodies), so I've kept copies
here.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER descriptions below are drafted from the documents' own

### [Patient Involvement at Cancer Research UK: Involve, Innovate, Inspire](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/cruk-patient-involvement-impact-report-2017.pdf) (2017)
A Cancer Research UK impact report on involving patients and the public in
the charity's research, campaigns and services.

### [Improving awareness of the English cancer registry](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/cruk-macmillan-cancer-registry-awareness-2016.pdf) (2016)
A Cancer Research UK / Macmillan review of how people with cancer are (and
should be) informed about the cancer registry, and their right to opt out.

### [Identification and handling of outlier institutions](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/ncin-outlier-handling-briefing-2012.pdf) (2012)
An NCIN briefing setting out principles for identifying and handling
"outlier" institutions when publishing comparative NHS data.

### [UK Funders' Vision for Human Tissue Resources](https://www.ukcrc.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/Vision+for+human+tissue+resources.pdf) (2011)
A joint MRC/NCRI report setting out UK research funders' shared five-year
vision for human tissue resources — consent, access and quality standards.

### [Building an e-health research infrastructure for cancer](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/ncin-ehealth-research-infrastructure-2011.pdf) (2011)
An NCIN consultation paper proposing new research infrastructure —
including a data "safe haven" and record-linkage services — for the
English cancer registry.

### [Evidence on cancer inequalities in England](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/ncin-cancer-inequalities-2010.pdf) (2010)
An NCIN review of how cancer incidence, survival and care vary by gender,
deprivation, age, ethnicity, sexuality, disability and religion.

### [Report of the Research Database Working Group](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/nigb-research-database-working-group-2010.pdf) (2010)
A report to the National Information Governance Board recommending
good-practice guidance for research databases holding NHS patient data.

### [Samples and Data for Research: Template for Access Policy Development](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/michaelchapman/doc_archive@main/ncri-access-policy-template-2009.pdf) (2009)
An NCRI/NCIN/onCore UK template to help research organisations develop
policies for sharing tissue samples and data with other researchers.
