      Sources

      The route shown for any monarch lists the sources behind each parent-to-child link. Some links were checked against the sources below. Most of the rest come from Wikidata, whose entry for each person is linked from the route, and the remainder follow the standard regnal genealogy. A handful of Wikidata links that historians reject or doubt were removed or marked as disputed by hand. None of these are primary records.

        Reign dates follow the conventional regnal lists. Disputed or uncrowned claimants (Empress Matilda, Lady Jane Grey, Edgar Ætheling) are not counted as monarchs.
