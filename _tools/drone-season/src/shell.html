DroneSeason

Two machine hives, one valley. Whatever is seen is killed, so the fight is over who sees first.

  • ScoutGlints see far and quietly. Perch one on a ridge to see further. Canopy hides what sits under it.
  • StrikeStings are cheap and spent on impact. Raiders bomb what they find, then fly home to rearm.
  • ProtectWardens patrol and net drones. Decoys look like Raiders to the rival and draw its fire.
  • LinkOrders reach drones near your Heart, Nests and Relays. Beyond that they carry on with what they were told. Harriers jam: drones near one fly blind.
  • HoldIn Last Light, the Crown bloom at the centre scores for whichever hive has more drones on it.

Win at frost on Crown score, or end it early by destroying the Cobalt Heart.

Your hive

Forager◆25Harvests nectar and brings it to the nearest base.
Glint◆10Quiet scout with long sight. 75-second battery.
Sting◆6Hits the first thing its seeker finds. Spent on impact.
Decoy◆5Reads as a Raider to the rival and draws its fire.
Warden◆35Patrols where you tap and nets drones. Three nets, then home.
Raider◆40Three bombs for clustered or still targets. Rearms at home.
Relay◆30Hovers where you send it and carries your link 300 further. Chains through other Relays in link.
Harrier◆45Jams rival drones within 200: they cannot report, take orders or use the hive's picture. Its noise gives it away: you can see one while it jams you.
Nest◆80Forward base and sensor. Drones launch from the nearest base. Carries your link too.

Commanding the hive

SquadsTap a squad, then tap the map or drag a route. Tap two to send them together. Long-press or right-click a squad to change its mix. Again resends your last squad the same way.
AutoKeeps two items in the queue from your plan: the first row below its target, saving up for it rather than skipping ahead. Losses are rebuilt. What you build yourself goes first.
PostsA spot the hive keeps staffed with Glints, Wardens or Decoys. It relieves a Glint before its battery fades and replaces Wardens that go home to rearm. Higher priority posts are filled first.
LinkNew orders reach only drones in link: near your Heart, a Nest, or a Relay in link. Out of link, a drone keeps its last orders until its battery says home. A post out of link takes a Relay out with it if one is ready.
JammingDrones near a rival Harrier are jammed. They cannot report what they see, take new orders or use the hive's picture, so Stings hunt on their own and Decoys fool them. A jammed post gets no relief. Kill the Harrier.
QuickTap a blue contact for Strike, Bomb or Intercept. Press and hold open ground to scout, hunt, patrol, jam, place a Relay, set a post or grow a Nest there.
