DroneSeason

Two machine hives, one valley. Whatever is seen is killed, so the fight is over who sees first.

  • ScoutGlints see far and quietly. Perch one on a ridge to see further. Canopy hides what sits under it.
  • StrikeStings are cheap and spent on impact. Raiders bomb what they find, then fly home to rearm.
  • ProtectWardens patrol and net drones. Decoys look like Raiders to the rival and draw its fire.
  • HoldIn Last Light, the Crown bloom at the centre scores for whichever hive has more drones on it.

Win at frost on Crown score, or end it early by destroying the Cobalt Heart.
